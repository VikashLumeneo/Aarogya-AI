Why AI Rx
The Problem

Handwritten prescriptions cause preventable harm

Illegible handwriting, missing information, and dangerous abbreviations lead to prescription errors that affect patient safety and create legal risk for physicians.

Illegible handwriting

Drug names, doses, and instructions can be misread by pharmacists, resulting in incorrect medications or dosing.

Missing critical information

Prescriptions may omit allergies, patient weight, administration routes, or follow-up instructions.

Dangerous abbreviations

Unsafe abbreviations and symbols can cause serious medication errors and compliance issues.

Undetected drug interactions

Without medication history visibility, harmful interactions may go unnoticed until adverse events occur.

The Arogya Solution

AI Rx eliminates every one of these risks

Generated directly from the clinical encounter, AI Rx produces structured, complete, and fully legible prescriptions every time.

Always legible, always structured

Digital generation eliminates handwriting errors and ensures medicines are presented in a standardized format.

Complete by default

Includes patient demographics, diagnosis, dosage, route, frequency, duration, allergies, and follow-up instructions.

Clear patient instructions

Uses plain-language directions instead of error-prone abbreviations, improving understanding and adherence.

Drug interaction checks

Reviews newly prescribed medications against existing treatment history before the prescription is finalized.

Generic-name compliance

Encourages standardized generic prescribing aligned with modern clinical and regulatory best practices.

Features

Everything a great prescription needs

Auto-Generated from AI Scribe

AI Rx is produced directly from the ambient clinical encounter recording — no manual entry required. The physician reviews and approves.

Drug Interaction Detection

Cross-references prescribed medications against the patient's medication history before the Rx is generated. Flagged interactions require physician acknowledgment.

Generic names, correct format

All medicines prescribed by INN generic name, in capitals, with correct spacing between drug name and strength — per MCI Professional Conduct Regulations.

Patient instructions & advice

Generates do's and don'ts, dietary guidance, and special instructions in plain language — and can display them in the patient's preferred Indian language.

Investigations & referrals included

Lab work, imaging referrals, and follow-up instructions are included on the same Rx document — so patients have everything they need in one place.

Multi-channel delivery

Rx delivered as a PDF to the patient's portal, or sent directly via WhatsApp or SMS — accepted at pharmacies, laboratories, and imaging centres.

Two-copy record keeping

Each Rx is automatically saved to the clinic EHR and sent to the patient's portal. Patients can forward the digital Rx to their pharmacy, show it on their phone, or download a PDF — no paper copies required.

Medication adherence reminders

After the Rx is sent, the platform can automatically remind patients via WhatsApp or SMS to take their medicines on schedule and complete prescribed investigations.

About Arogya Labs

Building the Intelligence Layer for Modern Healthcare

Arogya Labs is an AI healthcare technology company focused on helping clinicians work faster, document smarter, and deliver better patient care. We build intelligent systems that reduce administrative burden, improve clinical workflows, and assist healthcare professionals in making informed decisions with confidence.

Our Story

Arogya Labs exists to make healthcare work better for the clinicians who deliver it and the patients who depend on it.

Our story began with the Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences, an institution that has long embodied that belief, providing world-class care free of charge to all. The Institute gave us our north star: AI in service of people, the patients, physicians, and the communities they serve.

From capturing patient visits and generating electronic prescriptions to radiology intelligence and medication adherence reminders, Arogya Labs handles the complexity so clinicians can focus on care and patients can focus on their families, work, and life. We work across the full care journey: from the moment a patient has a symptom, consults our AI triage chat, to making an appointment with the right care provider in the same chat, to the moment a patient walks in, to the note in the chart, the prescription sent, the radiology report generated, and the follow-up that actually happens. All this while supporting more than 10 Indian languages to support easier communication for the patients when they need that comfort the most.

We design every product hand-in-hand with health systems, because we believe the people closest to care delivery understand it best. That partnership is how we build technology that works in the real world.

Our purpose is to put people at the center of health and social care. For patients, that means easier appointments, accurate prescriptions, and follow-ups that don't fall through the cracks. For care providers, it means clear, timely insights that help them do what they trained their whole lives to do: take care of people.
