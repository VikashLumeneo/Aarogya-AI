● About Anviq

Building the Intelligence Layer for Modern Healthcare

ANVIQ is an AI healthcare technology company focused on helping clinicians work faster, document smarter, and deliver better patient care. We build intelligent systems that reduce administrative burden, improve clinical workflows, and assist healthcare professionals in making informed decisions with confidence.

Our Story

ANVIQ exists to make healthcare work better for the clinicians who deliver it & the patients who depend on it.

Our story began with the Sri Madhusudan Sai Institute of Medical Sciences and Research, an institution that has long embodied that belief, providing world-class care free of charge to all. The Institute gave us our north star: AI in service of people, the patients, physicians, and the communities they serve.

From capturing patient visits and generating electronic prescriptions to radiology intelligence and medication adherence reminders, ANVIQ handles the complexity so clinicians can focus on care and patients can focus on their families, work, and life. We work across the full care journey: from the moment a patient has a symptom, consults our AI triage chat, to making an appointment with the right care provider in the same chat, to the moment a patient walks in, to the note in the chart, the prescription sent, the radiology report generated, and the follow-up that actually happens. All this while supporting more than 10 Indian languages to support easier communication for the patients when they need that comfort the most.

We design every product hand-in-hand with health systems, because we believe the people closest to care delivery understand it best. That partnership is how we build technology that works in the real world.

Our purpose is to put people at the center of health and social care. For patients, that means easier appointments, accurate prescriptions, and follow-ups that don't fall through the cracks. For care providers, it means clear, timely insights that help them do what they trained their whole lives to do: take care of people.

01 Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run our services and reply to you:

  • Information you give us: your name, work email, specialty, organisation and the plan you are interested in, when you fill in a form, request a demo, contact support or create an account.
  • Account and product data: login details and the content you create while using ANVIQ products (see section 3).
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic browser information, which our hosting provider records in standard server logs, and which we use briefly to block spam.

02 Website forms

This website has two forms. Here is exactly what happens when you submit them:

FormWhat we collectWhat happens
Request Early Access First and last name, work email, specialty, selected plan, the page you were on Sent by email to the ANVIQ team so we can contact you. You receive a confirmation email.
Notify me (Resources) Work email, the page you were on Added to our list to tell you when the Resources Hub launches. You receive a confirmation email.

To stop automated spam, our server briefly keeps the number of requests from each IP address in memory (for about 10 minutes). It is not stored or linked to your profile.

03 Healthcare & clinical data

ANVIQ products (Scribe, Rx, Radiologist and Receptionist) may process clinical notes, audio transcripts, prescriptions, imaging and related patient information on behalf of clinics and hospitals. For this data, the healthcare organisation decides what is collected and why. ANVIQ processes it only on their instructions, to deliver the service, and under a separate agreement with that organisation.

Patient data is never used for advertising and is never sold.

04 How we use information

  • Reply to early access, demo and support requests
  • Tell you about launches you asked to hear about
  • Provide, maintain and improve ANVIQ products
  • Keep the website and platform secure and prevent misuse
  • Meet our legal and regulatory obligations

We will not send you marketing emails you did not ask for. Every launch email lets you unsubscribe.

05 Sharing & service providers

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with providers that help us run our services, and only as much as they need:

Vercel: website hosting Google Workspace: email delivery Cloud infrastructure for ANVIQ products

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of our users and company.

06 Cookies & third-party content

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. To show fonts and icons, pages load files from Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare). When your browser downloads these files, those providers can see your IP address, as with any website request. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy first.

07 Security

Information is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Email passwords and keys are stored as protected server settings and never in the website code. Access to form submissions and product data is limited to people who need it. We review our safeguards regularly, but no system is completely risk-free.

08 Data retention

We keep form details only as long as we need them to respond to you and follow up on your request, or until you ask us to delete them. Product and clinical data is kept for as long as our agreement with the healthcare organisation and the law require.

11 Children

This website and our products are for healthcare professionals and organisations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

12 Changes to this policy
